Optimizing moves budget away from whichever channel currently has the lowest marginal ROI, the value of its next dollar, toward whichever has the highest, repeating until every channel's next dollar is worth about the same. That equal-marginal-return point is the mathematical optimum — not the channel with the best average ROI, the one whose curve still has room to climb.
Each channel's coefficient, half-saturation, and slope are fixed illustrative values for this demo — in the real system, all three come from the frozen posterior, one value per draw.
